GRIDLOCK

How to play

Goal. Slide every car off the board. Levels 1–3 teach the basics. Later boards are bigger, and each new twist shows up on its own level before they combine.

Moves. A car only travels the way its arrow points. It leaves if the path is clear and the exit matches its color. A bump costs a heart. Some cars are two cells long.

Later twists. Gray rocks block paths. A lock stays shut until the car it is chained to leaves. A small second square on an exit is the color it will switch to after the next car uses it. An exit marked 1 closes after one use. A circular arrow means that car changes color each time another car leaves.

Hearts and moves. Early levels give 3 hearts. From level 9 you get 2. From level 4 a move budget appears. Clearing the board within the perfect path is 3 stars. Run out of hearts or moves and the run ends.

Score. 100 points for each car that exits, plus a bonus for the level, hearts left, and stars.

Controls. Click a car to send it. Arrow keys move the selection. Enter or Space sends the selected car.
