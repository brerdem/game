How to play

Goal. Break the numbered blocks before one reaches the red line.

A turn. Aim and launch your colored balls. They bounce off the walls and the blocks. A block's number is its hit points. When the volley ends, every block drops one row and a new row appears at the top.

Color. A ball deals double damage to a block of the same color. Clear 3 blocks of one color in a single volley and a rainbow ball appears. It hurts every color and hits harder. A + pickup adds a ball of that color.

Surprise blocks. A dark block marked ? with a rainbow border shows up in some rows, more often later on. One appears in the opening rows. Break it and a toast names a random effect. About six in ten are helpful: extra balls, a bomb, row lightning, a card, bonus gems, a freeze, or a rainbow ball. The rest are a nuisance: extra blocks, tougher neighbors, a recolored ball, a slow next volley, a mini boss, or a wavering aim. None of them end the run by themselves.

Cards. Upgrades come only from ? blocks. Most card rolls hand you one card. A rarer roll lets you pick 1 of 3 after the volley. Offers lean toward tags you already hold. Cards cover blasts, paint, pierce, wall-bounce damage, color swap, extra balls, and cards that boost other cards. Held cards sit in a strip under the score. A starting card you equipped is already in that strip. A wide boss appears every 10 levels. The first one is meant to fall.

Unlocks. Rows and bosses pay gems, saved on this device. Spend them on new ball types (heavy, piercing, splitting) and new cards. Pick a ball and a starting card before the next run.

Lose. A block reaches the bottom line. Pickups do not.

Score. You score for damage dealt and for blocks you destroy. Bosses are worth more. Gems come from rows cleared and bosses broken.

Controls. Move the mouse to aim, then click or release to fire. Left and right rotate the aim, Up aims straight up, Space or Enter fires. On the cards, click one, or use the arrow keys and Enter.
