UNRAVEL

How to play

Goal. Clear the pixel picture. Each one you finish is replaced by a bigger picture with more colors.

A turn. You are dealt three colored spools. The number is how many pixels that spool can eat. It only reaches the bottom-most remaining pixel in each column, then the pixel above it once that one is gone.

Dock. Yarn left over parks in one of three slots. Use a docked spool later the same way. Clearing the picture spends the spool and loads the next one.

Lose. The dock is full and no spool in your hand or the dock can eat a pixel.

Score. Each pixel is worth 10 × your combo. The combo starts at 1 and rises every picture you clear, and finishing a picture adds a bonus. After a loss, the next deal is a little friendlier.

Controls. Click a spool. Left and right select, up and down move between your hand and the dock. Enter or Space plays the selected spool.
